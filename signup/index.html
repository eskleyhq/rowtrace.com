What happens next?

Eskley emails you a trial code, you download Rowtrace, and you paste the code in.

  1. Get your code

    Eskley reads each sign-up and emails a trial code to the address you gave. The code is one line of text and runs for 60 days.

  2. Download Rowtrace

    Get the code-signed program from the download page. There is nothing to install.

  3. Sign in

    Rowtrace opens on Sign in. Paste the code, then connect to Smartsheet with your API token.

Where do my details go?

Into a sheet in Eskley's own Smartsheet account.

The form asks for your company, your name, your email, a phone number and how many Smartsheet users you have. None of it goes into Rowtrace, and your Smartsheet token never goes to Eskley. What happens to the token is on Your token and your data.
