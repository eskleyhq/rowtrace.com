What happens next?

  1. Get your code

    Eskley reads each sign-up and emails a trial code and the download link. The code is one line of text and runs for 60 days.

  2. Download Rowtrace

    Use the link in the email. There is nothing to install.

  3. Sign in

    Rowtrace opens on Sign in. Paste the code, then connect to Smartsheet with your API token.

Where do my details go?

Into a sheet in Eskley's Smartsheet account. None of it goes into Rowtrace, and your Smartsheet token never goes to Eskley. What happens to the token is on Your token and your data.
